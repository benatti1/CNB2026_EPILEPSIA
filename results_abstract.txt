
RESULTS

A total of 320,419.0 epilepsy-related hospitalizations were analyzed across 27 Brazilian states (2021-2025), with an overall hospital mortality rate of 2.90%, mean cost of R$ 1,113.13 per admission, and average length of stay of 6.2 days.

K-Means clustering analysis (K=4) identified four distinct healthcare assistance profiles with a Silhouette Score of 0.316, indicating moderate cluster separation:

**Cluster 0 - Efficient Care - Lowest Mortality** (n=12 states):
Mortality: 2.11% | Mean Cost: R$ 912.15 | Mean LOS: 5.1 days
States: Rondônia, Amazonas, Pará, Maranhão, Ceará, Minas Gerais, Paraná, Santa Catarina, Rio Grande do Sul, Mato Grosso do Sul, Mato Grosso, Distrito Federal

**Cluster 1 - High Resource / High Mortality** (n=8 states):
Mortality: 3.55% | Mean Cost: R$ 1,279.12 | Mean LOS: 7.6 days
States: Roraima, Rio Grande do Norte, Paraíba, Sergipe, Bahia, Espírito Santo, São Paulo, Goiás

**Cluster 2 - Critical - Highest Mortality** (n=1 states):
Mortality: 6.93% | Mean Cost: R$ 2,349.08 | Mean LOS: 9.5 days
States: Alagoas

**Cluster 3 - Cost-Effective - Moderate Outcomes** (n=6 states):
Mortality: 3.46% | Mean Cost: R$ 713.12 | Mean LOS: 6.5 days
States: Acre, Amapá, Tocantins, Piauí, Pernambuco, Rio de Janeiro



These findings reveal significant regional disparities in epilepsy care across Brazil's public health system (SUS). The clustering analysis demonstrates that hospital mortality rates vary substantially among states, ranging from 2.11% to 6.93%, suggesting heterogeneous healthcare quality and resource allocation patterns.
